Our story

A studio built on care and craft

Lumière was founded by Elena Hofmann with a simple idea: beauty appointments should feel like a pause from the city, not another item on the to-do list.

Our team of seven specialists combines Viennese precision with a gentle, personal approach. We use premium, skin-friendly products, take time for a proper consultation, and never rush the result.
